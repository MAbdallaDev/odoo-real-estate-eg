================================
Real Estate Installments (Egypt)
================================

Installment sales and collections for Egyptian real-estate developers, built on
Odoo 19 Community.

A developer sells a unit on a payment plan: a down payment, years of scheduled
installments, a maintenance deposit due at delivery and late fees on overdue
amounts. This module turns that plan into a schedule, invoices each installment
on its due date through standard accounting, and gives the collections team the
lists they work from every day.

It covers contracts and their states, payment plans and whole-pound schedules,
invoicing through standard accounting, late fees computed from payment history,
collections screens, post-dated cheques, a bilingual Arabic / English contract PDF,
a unit import from CSV or Excel, and demo data for an invented developer.

**Status:** 1.0.0. Setup, accounting configuration, demo logins, screenshots and the
design notes are in the repository README:
https://github.com/MAbdallaDev/odoo-real-estate-eg
